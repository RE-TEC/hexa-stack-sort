Privacy Policy

Hexa Stack Sort: Color Puzzle · Effective date: November 20, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hexa Stack Sort Team ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you play the mobile game Hexa Stack Sort: Color Puzzle (the "App"), available on Google Play under the package name com.hexastacksort.colorpuzzle.

Contact for all privacy questions and requests: maximilian.mueller.altgolssen1@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your real name, email address or phone number.
  • Your game progress, settings, player name and optional profile photo are stored only on your device.
  • The App is free and financed by advertising through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised only if you agree in the consent dialog where this is required by law.
  • We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played (for example which levels are too hard), so we can improve it.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

The following data is saved locally in the App's private storage and is not uploaded to us:

  • Game progress (levels, stars, scores, coins, boosters, unlocked items, streaks and event progress).
  • Settings (language, sound, music, vibration, theme).
  • Your chosen player name, avatar and frame.
  • An optional profile photo. If you choose one, the App opens your device's photo picker, reduces the selected image to 150 × 150 pixels and stores it only on your device. The App does not access any other photos and does not upload the picture.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes this information permanently.

4. Third-party services

The App uses the following services provided by Google. They may collect information directly from your device under their own privacy policies.

4.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

AdMob shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads. To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, completed videos) and diagnostic data.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in other regions where it is required, see a consent dialog provided by Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App's settings ("Privacy settings"). If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4.2 Firebase Analytics (usage statistics)

Firebase Analytics records events about how the game is used, for example level started or completed, booster used, shop opened, ad watched or purchase completed, together with an app-instance identifier, device information, app version, language and country derived from the IP address. We use these statistics only in aggregated form to balance difficulty, fix problems and improve the game. Analytics data is not used to identify you personally. Where consent is required, analytics collection follows your choice in the consent dialog.

More information: Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4.3 Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Optional purchases such as coin packs, special offers or "Remove Ads" are handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment information; we only receive a confirmation of the purchase (product, order identifier and purchase status) so the App can deliver and restore the item.

More information: Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4.4 Sharing

If you use "Invite friends" or "Share", the App opens your device's standard share sheet. What you share and with whom is entirely your choice; the App does not read your contacts.

6. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised advertising and, where required, analytics.
  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for delivering purchased items.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalised ads that finance the free game, fraud prevention and keeping the App stable.

7. Data retention

Local game data stays on your device until you delete it. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for a maximum of 14 months and then deleted automatically. Retention by Google AdMob and Google Play is governed by Google's policies linked above.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Change or withdraw your ad consent at any time in the App's settings ("Privacy settings").
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Delete all local game data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data and the right to data portability. Contact us at the email address above; you may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we do not run our own servers or user accounts, we usually hold no personal data that could identify you. Requests about data held by Google can also be made directly to Google.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Communication with Google services is encrypted (HTTPS). Local data is stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access on a non-rooted device.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or legal requirements change. The current version is always available at this address, and the effective date above shows when it was last changed.
